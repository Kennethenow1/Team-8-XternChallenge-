# Interconnection procedure note

## Situation
The inquiry concerns the process and requirements at Decision Point II in the MISO Generator Interconnection Procedure. The project card is in the System Impact Study phase as of the observation date. This note restates BPM-015 r33 procedure for Decision Point II. It is not a legal opinion.

## Workflow
Once MISO delivers the revised System Impact Study (SIS) and Affected System analysis, including estimated upgrades and costs, the Interconnection Customer (IC) enters Decision Point II (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3). The IC shall have fifteen (15) Business Days to decide whether to proceed to Definitive Planning Phase III (DPP III) or withdraw its interconnection request (IR) (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3). During Decision Point II, the IC may reduce the size of its IR by up to ten percent (10%), but the DPP III Milestone (M4) calculation will be based on the DPP II results (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3). If the IC decides to proceed, it must pay the DPP III Milestone (M4) before the end of Decision Point II (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3).

If MISO does not receive written confirmation from the IC regarding its intent to proceed or withdraw during Decision Point II, MISO shall deem the IR withdrawn and refund the DPP II Milestone (M3) and any remaining study deposits pursuant to Section 7.6 of the GIP (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.5). After the IC enters DPP III, the DPP II Milestone (M3) becomes one hundred percent (100%) non-refundable (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.5).

The IC is eligible to receive a one hundred percent (100%) refund of the DPP II Milestone (M3) only if the IR is withdrawn or deemed withdrawn before the end of Decision Point II (BPM-015 r33 §6.2.11). If the IR is withdrawn after Decision Point II, the DPP II Milestone (M3) becomes at risk and will be used to fund Network Upgrades (BPM-015 r33 §6.2.11).

## Stakeholders
- The Interconnection Customer (IC) must decide whether to proceed or withdraw, may reduce IR size by up to ten percent (10%), and is responsible for milestone payments and potential withdrawal (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3).
- MISO (Transmission Provider) delivers the revised SIS and Affected System analysis, receives the IC's decision, and processes withdrawals and refunds as required (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3, §5.3.5, §6.2.11).
- The Transmission Owner (TO) and Affected System operators may be involved in the analysis and coordination of upgrades and impacts (BPM-015 r33 §5.3.3).
