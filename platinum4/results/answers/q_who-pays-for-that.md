The Interconnection Customer (IC) is responsible for funding the cost of a restudy if MISO determines that a restudy is required due to a peer interconnection request (IR) withdrawal. MISO will provide the IC with a notice that includes a good faith estimate of the restudy cost, and the IC must decide whether to proceed and fund the restudy or withdraw its IR (BPM-015 r33 §5.4.6).
