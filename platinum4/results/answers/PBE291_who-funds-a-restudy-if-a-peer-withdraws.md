# Interconnection procedure note

## Situation

You asked who funds a restudy if a peer Interconnection Request (IR) withdraws. This restates BPM-015 r33 and is not a legal opinion.

## Workflow

If MISO (the Transmission Provider) determines that a restudy is required due to the withdrawal of a peer IR before all Generator Interconnection Agreements (GIAs) are executed or filed unexecuted, MISO shall provide notice of the restudy, including a good faith estimate of the cost to perform the restudy (BPM-015 r33 §5.4.6). The Interconnection Customer (IC) shall notify MISO within five (5) Business Days whether it wishes to proceed with the restudy or withdraw its own IR. The cost to perform the restudy will be funded from any remaining study deposit or by an additional deposit as specified in the restudy notice (BPM-015 r33 §5.4.6).
