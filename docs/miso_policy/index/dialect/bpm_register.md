# Interconnection procedure note

## Situation
The inquiry concerns restudy funding after a peer withdrawal. The project card, if provided, is in the GIA or post-GIA phase as of the observation date. This note restates BPM-015 r33 procedure. It is not a legal opinion. It does not score compliance of a commercial operation date slip.

## Workflow
If MISO determines that restudy of an Interconnection Study is required because an interconnection request withdraws or is deemed withdrawn before all GIAs, FCAs, and/or MPFCAs for that DPP cycle have been executed or filed unexecuted with FERC, MISO shall provide notice of a restudy as necessary (BPM-015 r33 §5.4.6). The notice includes a preliminary analysis, an explanation of why restudy is required, and a good faith estimate of cost. The Interconnection Customer shall notify MISO within five (5) Business Days whether it wishes to proceed with the restudy or withdraw the IR. Failure to notify MISO is deemed withdrawal pursuant to Section 3.6 of the GIP. MISO will use reasonable efforts to complete the restudy within sixty (60) Calendar Days from the date of notice.

## Stakeholders
The Interconnection Customer funds restudy from remaining study deposit or from additional deposit as noticed. MISO notifies remaining interconnection requests and performs the restudy. The Transmission Owner coordinates affected facilities where the retrieved units name the TO.
