# 09 Expedite Resource Addition Study (ERAS)

9. Expedite Resource Addition Study (ERAS)

The ERAS process was approved by FERC on June 21, 2025.  It is a short-term solution with
strict eligibility requirements, designed to expedite the study and approval of generation needed
to address the region’s resource adequacy and/or reliability needs. Implementation of ERAS is
outlined in Attachment X and ERAS Informational Guide, posted on MISO website. ERAS will
sunset on the earlier of the completion of the 68th ERAS Interconnection Requests or on August
31, 2027.
