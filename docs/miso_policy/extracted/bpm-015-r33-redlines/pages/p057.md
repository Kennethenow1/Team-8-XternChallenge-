The Interconnection Customer may also request to change the legal name of their project
with a Name Change request - only applicable when the TAX ID is not changing.

5.5.1 Transfer of Ownership Required Documents
All documents must be submitted by the representative of the seller, included in the
Attachment C NDA list.

MISO will not accept a request sent by someone not included in the Attachment C NDA
list.

The Interconnection Customer must document the connection between the original owner
and the new owner. If the Interconnection Customer listed in the transfer documents
below as the original owner doesn’t match what MISO currently has on file, the request will
be rejected. The names must be consistent along with all the documents provided.

5.5.1.1 Standard Requirements
1. A Transfer Notice
a. A MISO Transfer Notice must be filled out and executed.
b. The correct customer’s name (seller) and buyer company (new customer
name) must be accurately listed
c. The MISO Transfer Notice template can be found on the MISO public
website.

2. Legal Documentation
a.  A legally binding, executed document that demonstrates proof of the
ownership for a transfer. The document must show the new entity name and
show evidence that a sale/transfer/assignment has taken place.

Accepted Documents
Notice of Sale
Purchase Agreement
Assignment of Interest Agreement
