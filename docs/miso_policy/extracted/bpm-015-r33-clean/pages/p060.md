5.5.3 How to Submit a Request
The Interconnection Customer may submit a transfer of ownership or name change request
in the MISO application portal.

The MISO Transfer of Ownership Guide contains images and detailed steps on how to submit
a request.

5.5.4 When the Interconnection Customer Can Submit a Request
5.5.4.1 DPP
The Interconnection Customer may submit a transfer of ownership and name change
request at any point in the queue process, but before the start of GIA negotiations. The name
of the Interconnection Customer on file, at the time of the negotiation start, will be used in
the draft GIA.

Transfer of ownership and name change requests will not be accepted after the GIA
negotiation window has started.

5.5.4.2 GIA
The Interconnection Customer can request transfer and GIA assignment any time after GIA
is executed.

5.5.5 Request Deliverables
Once the Interconnection Customer submits a transfer of ownership or name change
request, MISO will review and either accept or deny that request within 15 Business Days -
however if there is an issue, missing documents, or clarification needed with the
Interconnection Customer, the timeframe may be extended.

5.5.6 ERAS Transfer of Ownership
An Interconnection Customer participating in the Expedited Resource Addition Study may
transfer its Interconnection Request to another entity only after execution of the EGIA or
filing the EGIA unexecuted. The new entity must maintain the ERAS eligibility requirements.
